The Surprise Finding

🎯 The Real Bubble is in Infrastructure, Not Software

Direct AI companies (Microsoft, Google, OpenAI partners) underperformed the market, while semiconductor and infrastructure companies dramatically outperformed.

Top Performer: Micron (MU) +1002.21% | Worst Performer: C3.ai (AI) -57.11%

05

The Infrastructure vs Software Divide

❌ AI Software (Direct)

  • Underperformed S&P 500
  • Not yet monetized effectively
  • Public adoption still low
  • Business integration challenges
  • Skills gap limiting usage

✅ AI Infrastructure (Indirect)

  • Outperformed by 162%
  • Supplying the AI buildout
  • Immediate revenue from data centers
  • Semiconductor demand surge
  • Hardware sales booming
06

Why This Isn't a Bubble

  • Normal Tech Adoption Cycle: Infrastructure investment precedes software monetization (think railroads before railroad companies)
  • Fundamental Demand: Real infrastructure is being built—data centers, chips, energy capacity
  • Software Undervalued: AI software companies are actually UNDERperforming, not overvalued
  • Consulting Partnerships: OpenAI & Anthropic partnering with McKinsey, BCG to teach adoption—addressing the skills gap

Conclusion

Key Takeaway

The market is correctly pricing infrastructure needs now, while software monetization will follow as adoption grows. This is healthy, not bubbly.
